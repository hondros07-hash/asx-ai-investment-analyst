# V19.7 — MIA Research Score

Adds a transparent six-category 0–100 research scorecard:
Financial Quality, Growth, Valuation, Momentum, Balance Sheet & Risk, and Earnings & Thesis Trend.

Every category exposes its inputs, readings, points, evidence and source. Missing data
is N/A. Categories require minimum evidence and the overall score requires at least
four supported categories. The overall score is an equal-weight research summary, not
a Buy/Sell recommendation.

The scorecard appears prominently in Company Command Centre and is also available on
Research Home where the existing dashboard structure supports it.

Existing V19.6 Universal Symbol Search, V19.5.1 logo fallbacks, advanced forecasting,
analyst consensus, report intelligence, valuation and thesis monitoring are retained.
