# AXÍA V25.0 — Global Research & Consistent Methodology

Fifth moat foundation. Seven explicit market identities: ASX, NASDAQ, NYSE, LSE, HKEX, TSE and TSX. A normalized observation retains its native unit, currency, period, timestamp and HTTPS provenance. The comparison contract rejects mismatched units, metrics, periods and currencies. Missing observations stay unavailable.

GET /v1/global-methodology/markets, GET /identity/{exchange}/{ticker}, POST /compare. These are methodology/reference endpoints, not live exchange feeds or official-filing verification. Existing company data pipelines and widgets are unchanged.

Future integration: point-in-time issuer IDs and cross-listings, audited source adapters for each jurisdiction, fiscal calendar and accounting-standard mapping, verified dated FX conversions, evidence drawer and frontend display. No deployment or database migration in this PR.
