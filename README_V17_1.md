# V17.1 — Simplified Navigation

The previous long sidebar has been replaced by nine primary destinations:

- Home
- Markets
- Company Command Centre
- Before I Invest
- Monitor My Thesis
- Portfolio
- Trade Centre
- Research Tools
- Settings

Existing functionality is preserved and grouped inside contextual submenus:

Company Command Centre:
Overview, Fundamentals, Valuation, Technical, Announcements & Reports,
News & Events, Thesis Scorecard, Catalyst Calendar, Quant, Forecasts.

Portfolio:
Portfolio Overview, Portfolio Intelligence, Risk Centre, Watchlist, Paper Portfolio.

Trade Centre:
Trade Ticket, Orders, Strategy Builder.

Research Tools:
Research Report, Investment Committee, Evidence & Thesis, Model Lab.

Settings:
Workspace Settings, Data & Production, Broker Connections.

This is a navigation/UI refactor. It does not remove the underlying research engines.
