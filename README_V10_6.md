# V10.6 — Universal Sector & Peer Intelligence

Adds automatic peer and sector context to every company Dashboard.

Features:
- Detects sector and industry from company metadata.
- Searches a curated ASX or US liquid-company universe for comparable companies.
- Prioritises exact-industry peers, then same-sector peers.
- Displays peer prices and 1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y returns.
- Normalized relative-performance chart for 1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y / 3Y / 5Y.
- Equal-weight peer basket.
- Market benchmark comparison.
- Company-minus-peer and company-minus-market relative performance in percentage points.
- Works with ASX, NASDAQ and NYSE tickers supported by the existing data layer.

Important: automatic peer selection is a research aid. Sector/industry metadata can be imperfect and the peer group should be reviewed. The current seed universe is intentionally curated rather than claiming exhaustive exchange coverage.
